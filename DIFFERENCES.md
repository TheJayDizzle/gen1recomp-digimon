# Differences from the base game

Digimon in Kanto is an unfinished total-conversion-style demo built on
Pokémon Red and Blue. Its behavior intentionally differs from the base game
in the following major ways.

## Content and terminology

- Pokémon species are replaced with Digimon, including new stats, movesets,
  sprites, icons, Pokédex entries, encounters, and trainer parties.
- Interface text uses Digimon terminology, including Tamer, Digimon, and
  Digidex.
- The demo currently ends near the beginning of Nugget Bridge in Cerulean
  City, where an NPC prevents further progress.

## Progression

- Automatic level-based evolution is replaced by a manual Digivolution and
  De-Digivolution menu with branching paths and individual requirements.
- Digivolving or De-Digivolving resets the Digimon to level 1, recalculates
  its stats, and resets its moves for the new form.
- De-Digivolution can award permanent bonus stats based on the level reached.
- The experience curve is reduced to support repeated training cycles.

## Battles, moves, and items

- New types and moves are available, and some existing moves are modified.
- TMs can be reused instead of being consumed after one use.
- The battle interface supports larger, true-color Digimon sprites and custom
  placement for front and back sprites.
- The shortcut that selects RUN with the B button is disabled in trainer
  battles.

## Interface

- Party, item-target, battle-switch, status, Digidex, and Digivolution screens
  include custom layouts and higher-resolution Digimon artwork.
- Party borders use Digivice-themed corner graphics.
- Digidex entries can be changed with the Up and Down controls.

## Compatibility and saves

- The mod supports Pokémon Red and Pokémon Blue only.
- Pokémon Yellow is not supported.
- A new save is recommended. Save compatibility and balance may change during
  development.
